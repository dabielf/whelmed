# Whelmed

Whelmed helps a person take small or large daily actions that express their chosen ACT values. Goals stay visible to help the person choose useful actions without replacing the values.

## Language

**Value**:
An ongoing, chosen quality of action with a short name and an optional personal meaning. A Value gives direction but cannot be completed; it may change or be deleted as the person's understanding develops.

**Active Value**:
A Value currently included in the daily practice. It appears in the Daily Log immediately when created or restored, including today, and asks for at least one completed Daily Action each day.

**Paused Value**:
A saved Value excluded from the daily practice without being deleted. It does not make a day incomplete.

**Deleted Value**:
A Value removed from current use together with its Action Menu. Past Daily Actions remain in History under the Value's last name.

**Goal**:
A finishable outcome kept as a friendly reminder. An unfinished Goal in Week, Month, Year, or Someday appears on the dashboard; it is not linked to Values or Daily Actions.

**Goal List**:
A collection of Goals for the Week, Month, Year, or Someday horizon. Each list may contain several Goals, including several shown together on the dashboard.

**Completed Goal**:
A Goal marked finished and removed from active Goal Lists and the dashboard. It remains in a closed Completed Goals section and can be restored.

**Needs Review**:
A holding area for unfinished Goals whose time period has ended. Each Goal keeps the Week, Month, or Year it came from until the person moves, completes, or deletes it.

**Upcoming Goal**:
A Goal assigned to a future Week, Month, or Year. It waits outside the dashboard until its period begins, then joins the matching current Goal List.

**Value-aligned Action**:
A concrete action, small or large, that expresses a Value.
_Avoid_: Task

**Daily Action**:
A Value-aligned Action attached only to today, never scheduled for a future day or added to a past day. It starts with one Value and can visibly be added to other Values without that extra step being required. It can be planned earlier today or logged after it is done that day, and it counts only when marked Done. An unfinished Daily Action disappears when the day ends; all completed Daily Actions count equally, and the app does not store their size or effort.

**Daily Log**:
The day view of every Active Value, including Values with no Daily Action yet. Each Value is a direct starting point for adding, viewing, and completing its Daily Actions.

**App Time Zone**:
The Time Zone used to decide Today and when a Daily Log becomes History across devices. The person may choose it in Settings; until then, each browser supplies its Time Zone and Settings reminds the person to confirm one.

**History**:
The read-only record of completed Daily Actions from past days. It can show how many completed Daily Actions each Value has over a chosen date range, without adding streaks or scores. A completed Daily Action linked to several Values adds one to each Value's count; unfinished actions never appear.

**Action Menu**:
A reusable list of Value-aligned Actions saved under a Value for times when choosing an action is hard. A Daily Action may come from this menu or be entered freely; a free entry may be saved to the menu for later reuse.

**Action Menu Entry**:
A reusable Value-aligned Action saved under exactly one Value. Using it copies its wording into an independent Daily Action, so later edits or removal do not change existing Daily Actions.
